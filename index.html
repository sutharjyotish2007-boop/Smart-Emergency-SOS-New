BE PREPARED

Quick safety guides

✚

Medical

  • Call emergency services for serious or life-threatening conditions.
  • Follow the call handler's instructions.
  • Do not give food or drink to an unconscious person.
♨

Fire / smoke

  • Leave if you can do so safely; use stairs, not lifts.
  • Call from a safe place.
  • Do not re-enter until authorities say it is safe.
🚧

Road accident

  • Keep away from traffic and other hazards.
  • Call for help and give a clear location.
  • Avoid moving a seriously injured person unless there is immediate danger.
🌦

Disaster

  • Follow official alerts and evacuation instructions.
  • Stay away from floodwater and downed power lines.
  • Check on others only if it is safe.
